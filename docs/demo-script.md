# Demo Script (2–5 minutes)

1. **Open the dashboard.** "MPLAD covers thousands of small projects — reviewing each manually isn't feasible. This screen gives leadership a single view of the portfolio and where risk is concentrated." Point out: total projects, total sanctioned value, high & critical risk counts, delayed projects, financial anomalies, data quality issues.
2. **Show the risk distribution pie and anomaly frequency bar.** "Most of the portfolio is low or medium risk — the system focuses attention on the small tail that needs it."
3. **Show the state map.** "High-risk concentration in Rajasthan stands out because of a Jaipur cluster we'll come back to."
4. **Jump to the top high-risk table.** Click **MPLAD-01012 — Community Hall works at Jaipur Central**.
5. **On the investigation page**, walk through:
   - The **risk gauge**: 100/100 CRITICAL.
   - The **risk signals table**: five signals, each with its own weight and explanation — cost anomaly, expenditure over-run, timeline anomaly, severe delay, near-duplicate.
   - The **cost vs peer median** and **duration vs peer median** charts.
   - The **nearest comparable projects** table.
   - The **relationships graph** (agency, location, vendor, peers).
   - The **recommended investigation steps** — verifying rates, revised estimates, site inspection reports, duplicate billing.
6. **Open the Ask-AI page.** Ask "*Why is MPLAD-01012 high risk?*" The assistant answers with the grounded plain-language explanation.
7. **Ask** "*Which agencies have the highest anomaly rates?*" — the assistant lists agencies with average risk scores.
8. **Back on the project page**, click **Generate Investigation Report (PDF)**. Open the downloaded file to show project info, risk score, signals table, peer comparison, recommendations and the disclaimer.
9. **Open the Map view.** Point out the dense colour-coded cluster near Jaipur.
10. **Open the Agencies view.** "Two agencies dominate the top of the risk list — a natural place to open a portfolio-level audit."

## Talking points

- Terminology: risk indicators requiring verification, not proven fraud.
- LLM ≠ anomaly detector. Statistics for the signals, language for the explanation.
- Every signal shows why it fired — auditable end-to-end.
- The system fails safely: if the LLM is unavailable, the deterministic answerer keeps the demo working.
